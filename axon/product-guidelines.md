# Product Guidelines: KopaWee

## Brand Identity & Tone
- **Voice:** Encouraging, trustworthy, energetic, and culturally resonant with Nigerian youth.
- **Tone:** Practical, clear, and action-oriented during administrative procedures; warm and community-driven in social and marketplace interactions.

## Design System & Visual Aesthetics
- **Theme:** Vibrant modern interface supporting dark and light modes seamlessly.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary Brand Accent: Emerald Green (`#059669` / `#008751`) reflecting youth & national service pride.
  - Secondary Accents: Warm Amber (`#D97706`) for notifications and safety alerts; Slate (`#0F172A`) for deep surfaces.
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif stack (`Inter`, `Geist`, or system default) with clear hierarchy and readable line spacing.
- **Components & Effects:** Subtle glassmorphism cards, micro-animations on interactive actions, clear badge indicators, and high contrast for outdoor legibility.

## UX Principles
1. **Proactive over Passive:** Deliver timely alerts, calendar syncs, and reminders rather than forcing users to pull information.
2. **Mobile-First & Offline-Ready:** Critical items (call-up letters, green card PDFs, SOS emergency numbers) must be cached and accessible without internet connection.
3. **Safety & Trust First:** Verified corper badges for marketplace transactions and accommodation listings.
