# Product Definition: KopaWee (NYSC Companion)

## Vision
**KopaWee** is an active, modern personal assistant and companion application designed specifically for National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) members across Nigeria. While official NYSC portals function strictly as passive administrative databases, KopaWee acts as an indispensable, proactive companion from the moment a graduate receives their call-up letter until long after passing out (POP).

## Target Audience
1. **Prospective Corps Members (PCMs):** Preparing for mobilization, call-up, and orientation camp.
2. **Serving Corps Members:** Navigating daily PPA life, monthly LGA clearance, CDS activities, travel, housing, and budgeting.
3. **Outgoing / Ex-Corps Members:** Selling relocation/POP items, searching for post-service jobs/gigs, and alumni networking.
4. **Employers & PPAs (Places of Primary Assignment):** Onboarding corps members and providing rating/feedback.

## Key Feature Pillars

### 1. Smart Clearance & Admin Assistant
- **Proactive Notifications:** Automatic alerts for LGA clearance dates, call-up updates, and relocation approvals instead of manual portal checks.
- **Calendar & Travel Sync:** One-tap Google Calendar integration, LGA office map navigation, traffic time estimates, and document checklist.

### 2. Peer-to-Peer Corper Marketplace
- **Direct Buying & Selling:** Secure marketplace for incoming and outgoing corps members to buy, sell, or swap items (mattresses, gas cylinders, fans, cooking utensils, electronics).
- **POP Deals:** Dedicated listings for passing-out corpers handing off household setups to new corpers in the same LGA/State.

### 3. Accommodation & Roommate Matching
- **Corper Housing Directory:** Listings for corper-friendly apartments and lodge rentals near PPAs and LGA centers.
- **Roommate Finder:** Preference-based matching (gender, LGA, PPA proximity, budget) to split rent with fellow corps members.

### 4. Pre-Camp & Orientation Camp Guide
- **Offline Document Vault:** Encryption-backed local storage for call-up letters, green cards, medical certificates, and passport photos.
- **Camp Survival Kit:** Packing checklists, camp rules, platoon advice, and CDS selection guides.

### 5. Workplace (PPA) & CDS Hub
- **PPA Navigation & Onboarding:** Route planning, contact details, and employer rating/reviews.
- **CDS Group Manager:** Meeting reminders, attendance tracking, and community project collaboration.

### 6. Emergency & Travel Safety Tracker
- **Trip Check-In:** Active status reporting for long-distance highway travel between home state, camp, and PPA.
- **SOS Alert System:** Quick emergency broadcast to trusted contacts and local corper reps.

### 7. Post-POP Career & Gig Marketplace
- **Career Launchpad:** Integration with NiYA job bank, local gig boards, CV builder, and alumni networking.
